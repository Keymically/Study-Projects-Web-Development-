My Favourite Games I've played in 2025

The Top 5 Videogames (so far) according to Me! unordered cuz who cares about rankings

Baldur's Gate 3

the most pure D&D experience I've ever had in a video game. my main way of having fun and spending my free time for about 6 months of this year.

God of War Ragnarok

a rare example of AAA game worth the huge budget poured into it. left me in tears wanting to be a better version of myself.

Outer Wilds

true adventure and mystery in every small detail everywhere. so calm yet scary, so smart, and just so different.
not for everyone, but for me it hit hard.
even made me have a lot of real life thinking about the meaning of things around me in life

Hollow knight: Silksong

such a perfect videogame. a metroidvania with simple controls gorgeous art, beautiful music and tight controls for challanging levels and bosses.
pure fun all the time.

Doom The Dark Ages

another great shooter will all the speedy action while listening to great music.
its another modern doom game not to similar and not to different then the other two we got since 2016.
run around and slay demons with lots of weapons and no bullshit. Doom games are great!
