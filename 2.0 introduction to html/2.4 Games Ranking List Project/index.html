My Favourite Games I've played in 2025

The Top 3 Games (so far) according to Me! unordered cuz who cares about rankings

Baldur's Gate 3

the most pure D&D experience I've ever had in a video game. my main way of having fun and spending my free time for about 6 months of this year.

God of War Ragnarok

a rare example of AAA game worth the huge budget poured into it. left me in tears wanting to be a better version of myself.

Outer Wilds

true adventure and mystery in every small detail everywhere. so calm yet scary, so smart, and just so different.
not for everyone, but for me it hit hard.
even made me have a lot of real life thinking about the meaning of things around me in life
